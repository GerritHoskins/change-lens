# ChangeLens privacy policy

> **DRAFT 2026-11-13, not legally reviewed.**

Last updated: 2026-11-13

ChangeLens is an app for iOS and Android that compares two versions of a brief and shows what changed, with evidence from both documents. This policy explains which personal data we process when you use the app, why, for how long, and what rights you have.

## 2. Summary

- You need an account. We store your email address and, if you use them, your Apple or Google sign-in.
- The documents you import and the comparisons we create are stored on servers in Germany. They are deleted 30 days after you last use them, or earlier if you delete them.
- To compare documents, we send their text to an AI model run by IONOS in Germany. The model provider does not get your name or email address.
- We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party tracking or analytics tools.
- We count how the service is used, on our own server, to improve the app and decide on future pricing. Additional in-app usage statistics are collected only if you agree.

## 3. Data we process, purposes and legal bases

### 3.1 Your account

**Data:** email address; for Sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier the provider sends us and, for Apple, an encrypted token we need to revoke the connection when you delete your account; sign-in and security events (time, success or failure, MFA status); the fair-use allowance you have used.

**Purpose:** to create and secure your account, sign you in, and enforce the fair-use allowance.

**Legal basis:** performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Security events: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and your account (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Sign-in codes are sent by email through IONOS.

### 3.2 Documents and comparisons

**Data:** the text or PDF files you import (including any personal data they contain, such as names and contact details of your clients), the project names and client labels you enter, the text we extract, the findings, quotes and explanations of a comparison, and your reviews, corrections, notes and client-approval records.

**Purpose:** to provide the comparison you request and keep it available to you.

**Legal basis:** performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If a document contains personal data of other people, we process it on your behalf and on your instructions; please make sure you are allowed to share it with us.

The app keeps no copy of your documents on your phone after import. Imported files are held in memory only while you use them.

### 3.3 AI processing

To find changes, we send selected text from your documents to a language model on the IONOS AI Model Hub in Germany. We send document text only, never your name, email address or account details. The model's answer is checked against your documents before we show it; findings that cannot be matched to a quote are withheld. The app does not make decisions about you (Art. 22 GDPR). Findings can be wrong or incomplete; always check them against the documents.

### 3.4 Usage measured on our server

**Data:** events derived from requests the app already sends to provide the service: account created, comparison created and completed, review saved (including whether the evidence was shown in the PDF or as text, and whether the page was opened), allowance reached, and the day you were last active. These events are linked to an internal account number, not to your email address in reports. They never contain document content.

**Purpose:** to understand whether the app is useful, find problems, plan capacity and costs, and decide on future plans and pricing.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in improving and financing the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). No information is read from or stored on your device for this purpose. You can object at any time (section 8); we will then exclude your account from these statistics.

### 3.5 Optional in-app usage statistics

Only if you agree: the app sends us events about how you use it, for example which screens you open and which report filters you use. These go to our own server, never to a third party, and never include document content.

**Legal basis:** your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The setting is off until you turn it on. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app under [SETTINGS PATH]; this does not affect processing before withdrawal.

### 3.6 Feedback and survey questions

If you send feedback or answer an optional question in the app (for example, what the app is worth to you), we store your answer with your account number.

**Demo feedback form (draft, added 2026-11-22, not legally reviewed):** during the demo period the app offers one feedback form per account. If you send it, we store with your account number: which predefined answer you chose for what is missing, which monthly amount you chose, the plan description that question referred to, the time, and what you typed into the two text fields ("Other" and "Anything else?"). The text fields are stored as you wrote them. Please do not enter text from your documents or personal data about other people there. The price question is research; nothing is offered or charged. Sending the form does not change what you can use in the app.

**Legal basis:** your consent, given by answering (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Answering is never required. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm consent as the basis for the demo feedback form, and how a single answer is withdrawn; today it is deleted with the account or on request to contact@pixeltronic.dev.]

### 3.7 Server logs

**Data:** time, route, status code, error type, the IP address of the request, and internal IDs. Logs never contain document content.

**Website (changelens.pixeltronic.dev):** for each page view we log the time, the page, the website that linked to us (host name only), campaign parameters (`utm_…`) and whether the request came from a crawler. We do not log your IP address, your browser, or any cookie, and we do not count unique visitors. Log lines are deleted after 30 days; daily totals without personal data are kept. 

**Visit statistics (website):** we also run Umami, a statistics tool on our own server; no data goes to a third party. A small script from our own host reports each page view and clicks on a few elements (the early-bird buttons, the scope answers, the language switch). From your IP address and browser identifier Umami computes a visitor code with a key that changes every day, so a visitor can't be recognised on a later day; the IP address itself is not stored. It also stores the country, the browser and the device class. It sets no cookie. If your browser sends "Do Not Track", nothing is recorded. [DRAFT 2026-11-22: switched on by the owner's decision before the legal review (L4) has answered whether this needs consent; legal basis and retention period are still to be set.]

**Purpose:** to run the service securely and find errors. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 3.8 Early-bird list (website)

> **Draft paragraph added 2026-11-22 with the early-bird endpoint; not legally reviewed, like the rest of this draft. The list is open since 2026-11-23 by the owner's decision.**

If you enter your email address in the early-bird form on our website, we store the address, the language of the page and the time. We then send one email with a confirmation link to that address (double opt-in). Only after you open the link and press the confirm button on the page is your address on the list; we store the time of the confirmation as proof of your consent. To limit misuse of the form we also keep, for up to 3 days, counters keyed with a non-reversible keyed hash of the IP address (for IPv6, of its network) and of the email address, and a count of all confirmation emails sent; none of these counters contains an address.

**Purpose:** to tell you when the full version of ChangeLens is available and to give you the early-bird discount. An address on the list is not an account and is not needed to use the app.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for the emails also § 7(2) UWG). You can withdraw it at any time through the unsubscribe link in every email to the list, or by writing to contact@pixeltronic.dev. With the link your address is deleted at once, and we keep no record that it was on the list; if you write to us we delete it then. Copies in backups expire as described in section 6. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it.

**Retention:** an address that is not confirmed is deleted 7 days after the last confirmation email. A confirmed address is kept until you withdraw your consent or we close the list.

## 9. Is providing data required?

You need an email address or an Apple or Google account to use ChangeLens, and you need to import documents to compare them. Everything in sections 3.5 and 3.6 is optional.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Each account's data is separated from other accounts in the database. Document content is kept out of logs, error reports and statistics. Sign-in tokens are stored in your phone's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore).
