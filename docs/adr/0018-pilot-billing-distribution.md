# ADR 0018: Pilot billed by direct invoice; distributed through test tracks

**Status:** Accepted (owner decision) (2026-10-30)

## Context

App-store in-app purchase takes 15–30% and the rules for B2B subscriptions are unverified.

## Decision

Pilot accounts are invoiced directly and provisioned by the operator. Apps are distributed through TestFlight and Play closed testing. No in-app purchase in the pilot.

## Consequences

Store payment rules must be resolved before any public listing.
