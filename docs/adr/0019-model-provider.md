# ADR 0019: Model provider selection

**Status:** Proposed: open until D-7 (2026-10-30)

## Context

Retention, training use, EU processing, DPA and price are all unverified.

## Decision

Pick from the D-7 comparison of two providers: no training on inputs, zero or short retention (including abuse monitoring), EU or UK processing.

## Consequences

Blocks I-2 and the pilot data terms (D-4).
